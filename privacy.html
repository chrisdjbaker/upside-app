Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the Upside app for iPhone and iPad (“the app”).

We don’t collect your data

The developer does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no servers.

Your city

Upside never asks for your location. You type the city you want, and it is saved only on your device. If you search for a town that isn't on Upside's list, its name is looked up with Apple's map service to find where it is. That lookup is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Reading the news

To show stories, your device downloads public news feeds and articles directly from the newsrooms that publish them, the same way a web browser would. Those newsrooms can see an ordinary web request from your device. Once a day the app also downloads an updated list of newsrooms from this website (GitHub Pages). None of these requests include your city or any personal information from the app.

Summaries and good-news checks

On devices with Apple Intelligence, stories are checked and summarized by Apple's on-device model. This happens on your device and nothing is sent to the developer.

What stays on your device

Your places, settings, saved stories, journal entries, scores and the latest stories are stored on your device. The widgets and Siri use the same data on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your device. No push server is used.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
