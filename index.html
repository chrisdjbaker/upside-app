Upside

Real good news from where you live. Upside reads local newsrooms' own feeds, keeps the uplifting stories, and sums them up for you, with a link to every original.

Get help or report a problem

Open a ticket on the support page on GitHub. Tell us your device, iOS version, your city and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Where do the stories come from? From the public news feeds of local TV stations, newspapers, public radio and nonprofit newsrooms, plus a few outlets that only cover good news. Upside never writes or changes stories. Every story links to the newsroom that published it.

How does Upside decide what's good news? On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the on-device model reads each headline and description. On other iPhones, Upside uses its own built-in checks. Nothing is sent to a server for this.

Which cities are covered? Over 80 cities in the US, Canada, the UK, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand. You can follow any town: Upside uses the covered cities within the range you pick. Want your city added? Open a ticket on the support page.

What is the Upside score? The share of your area's local stories from the last two days that are good news. The Score tab shows how it changes over 30 days.

How do I hide stories I don't want? In Settings, add muted words, or turn off a newsroom under Muted sources. You can also touch and hold any story and choose Mute.

How does Listen work? Tap the headphones on the Today screen. Upside reads the day's stories aloud and keeps going when your screen locks. You can also say “Hey Siri, listen to Upside.”

What can I ask Siri? “Get good news from Upside”, “Listen to Upside”, “Add a good thing to Upside” and “What's my Upside score”. They're also in the Shortcuts app.

How do I add the widgets? Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, then add Upside. The Home Screen widget shows 3 Good Things; the Lock Screen widgets show the top story or your score.

Why didn't my morning reminder show new stories? iOS decides when apps refresh in the background. If Upside hasn't had a chance to check the news, the reminder still comes and the stories load when you open the app.
